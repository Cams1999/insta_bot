Instructions

1. Go to Accounts and add an Instagram account.

2. Click "Test Connection" to verify the login works.

3. Click "Start Bot", enter a target account (e.g. "cristiano"), and set how many followers to follow.

4. The bot runs in the background. Monitor progress on the account detail page.

5. Configure limits, delays, and probabilities per account in the config section.

Safety Tips

Use conservative limits to avoid Instagram action blocks:

  • Start with 50-80 follows/day for the first week
  • Gradually increase to 100-150 over 2-3 weeks
  • Use a mobile 4G/5G proxy for best results
  • Keep your account active with regular posts and stories
  • Enable warm-up mode for accounts that haven't been active recently
